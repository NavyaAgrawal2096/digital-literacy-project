## Stay Safe Online: Prevention Checklist

- Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, or banking password with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank or the police.
- Do not scan random QR codes to “receive” money. In UPI apps, scanning a QR code usually sends money, not receives it.
- Check every UPI payment request carefully before entering your PIN. If you did not expect the request, reject it immediately.
- Use strong, different passwords for email, banking, social media, and college accounts. A password recycled across websites is like using one key for your hostel room, bike, and locker. One theft opens every door.
- Turn on two-factor authentication for Gmail, Instagram, banking apps, and important accounts.
- Avoid using public Wi‑Fi for online banking, shopping, or entering passwords. Open Wi‑Fi is a digital tea stall where strangers may be listening to every conversation.
- Download apps only from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Never install APK files sent through WhatsApp, Telegram, or unknown links.
- Verify job offers, internship links, scholarship forms, and “urgent” messages before clicking. College students are common targets because scammers know many are looking for internships, discounts, and side-income opportunities.
- Keep your phone, laptop, browser, and antivirus updated. Updates are not decoration. They are tiny armor plates hammered onto your devices.
- If your account is hacked or money is stolen, report it immediately at cybercrime.gov.in and call Helpline 1930. Speed matters. A delayed report gives the scammer a longer head start through the digital maze.
